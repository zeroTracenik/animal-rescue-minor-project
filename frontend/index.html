How It Works

From spot to recovery: how technology connects compassionate citizens with local field teams in four easy steps.

1
1. Report

Citizen captures an animal in need, shares live GPS coordinates, condition and a photo without needing to register.

2
2. Find Nearby Rescue

Our Haversine spatial algorithm compares report coordinates with registered NGOs to automatically find the nearest facility.

3
3. Volunteer Assigned

NGO reviews triage priority and dispatches an available field responder equipped with carrier or medical kit.

4
4. Animal Rescued

The animal receives veterinary care, wound dressing, shelter and rehabilitation with transparent status tracking.
