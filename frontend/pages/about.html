About The Project

Animal Rescue Connect

“One Report Can Save a Life.”

What is Animal Rescue Connect?

Animal Rescue Connect is an animal welfare and rescue coordination platform designed to bridge the gap between citizen bystanders, shelter hospitals, and certified field responders.

Every day, street dogs, cats, cows, and birds suffer from road accidents, sickness, or entrapment. Often, citizens want to help but do not know which NGO operates in that neighborhood, how to describe the emergency severity, or whether help is truly en route. Animal Rescue Connect introduces a structured digital pipeline from the moment a photo is taken to hospital treatment.

Our Goal & Workflow

Our goal is to make reporting effortless for citizens while providing emergency triage and accountability for rescue organisations.

Fast Frictionless Reporting

Citizens can file an emergency report in seconds with automatic browser geolocation without mandatory account creation.

Spatial Matching

The Haversine distance formula calculates the closest verified rescue center based on GPS latitude and longitude.

Structured Progression

A 6-step lifecycle: Reported ➔ Assigned ➔ On the Way ➔ Rescued ➔ Treatment ➔ Completed ensures full operational transparency.

Citizen Privacy Protection

Private reporter telephone numbers are never exposed on public feeds, safeguarding citizen confidentiality.

Rescue Priority Framework

To prevent ambulance bottlenecks, reports are triaged into four organizational categories:

CRITICAL PRIORITY

Immediate life threat / trauma requiring ambulance within minutes.

Examples: Open trauma, hit-and-run collision, severe hemorrhage, suspected spinal or pelvic fracture.
URGENT PRIORITY

Severe distress requiring prompt dispatch within a few hours.

Examples: Deep puncture wounds, severe maggot infection, trapped in drain, extreme heat exhaustion/dehydration.
MODERATE PRIORITY

Non-immediate medical assistance or scheduled vet visit.

Examples: Chronic skin disease (mange/alopecia), mild limp, malnutrition, eye discharge.
LOW / SHELTER CARE

Non-emergency rehabilitation and shelter support.

Examples: Abandoned newborn litter needing foster care, routine adoption or vaccination inquiries.
Important Medical Clarification: These priority categories are for dispatch triage and organization, and do not replace on-site veterinary assessment.

Rescue Operations & Safety Protocols

Animal Rescue Connect operates with a community-first approach, adhering to humane animal handling standards and safety procedures for both citizens and rescue responders:

  • Location Precision: Always provide exact landmarks or use the GPS button to ensure responders can locate animals quickly.
  • Safety First: Never approach highly aggressive or panicked animals without proper protective gear or handler assistance.
  • Confidentiality: Citizen contact details are shared exclusively with the responding rescue volunteer to coordinate victim retrieval.
  • Ambulance Support: For critical road trauma, our partner network dispatches equipped hydraulic ambulances and portable stretcher carriers.

For urgent dispatch inquiries or immediate assistance, our helpline is available 24/7 at 1800-RESCUE-00.
